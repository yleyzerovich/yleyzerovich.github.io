Terms of Use: ADA (Acoustic Drone Alert)

Last updated: 2026-10-22 Contact: aimotioncoach@gmail.com

1. What ADA is

ADA listens, on your phone, for sound patterns that resemble a drone, and tells you when it thinks it has heard one. Detection happens on the device. Optional saved locations describe where the phone heard an observation. You can deliberately open or export selected data to another app. Optional Telegram notifications can send acoustic alert facts to a destination you configure; optional technical JSON attachments provide measurements for inspection and future comparison. Location inclusion is a separate choice and audio is not attached. Reports can also be saved or shared directly from History without Telegram, using only saved data. Older records may lack acoustic evidence; bounded exports disclose omitted windows or passes. Local report capture and location inclusion are optional independent choices. External copies remain controlled by the selected destination. Technical reports, model hypotheses and embeddings do not establish an individual drone identity or a calculated drone position.

2. What ADA is not: please read this part

ADA is an indicative acoustic detector. It is not a security system, not a safety system, and not a countermeasure. Do not rely on it for anything where being wrong matters.

Specifically, and honestly:

  • It will miss drones. Wind, traffic, rain, distance, an unusual aircraft, a quiet one, or one approaching from downwind can all mean ADA hears nothing at all.
  • It will sometimes alert when there is no drone. Fans, HVAC, lawnmowers, distant traffic and other steady machinery can resemble a rotor.
  • A model name is a guess. When ADA names a drone model it is reporting similarity to recordings it was trained on. It is frequently "Unknown", it can be wrong, and it says nothing about who is flying, from where, or why.
  • It cannot locate a drone. Optional location tagging records the observing phone, not source direction, distance, altitude or an aircraft track.
  • It only works while armed, with the microphone available to it. Another app, a phone call, or your phone's battery manager can stop it, and it will not restart itself.
  • Detection quality varies by device, microphone, environment and app version.
  • Telegram delivery is not guaranteed. Messages can be delayed, missed, duplicated or uncertain. An acoustic alert does not confirm a physical drone. Peer confirmation, matching and triangulation are not provided by this notification feature.

You must not use ADA as the basis for decisions affecting personal safety, security, property, legal action, or compliance with any regulation. If a drone matters to you that much, use equipment built and certified for the purpose.
