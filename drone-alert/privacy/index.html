Privacy Policy — ADA (Acoustic Drone Alert)

Last updated: 2026-09-23
Applies to: ADA — Acoustic Drone Alert for Android
Contact: aimotioncoach@gmail.com

The short version

ADA listens for the sound of a drone on your phone, and only on your phone.

  • Audio from your microphone is analysed as it arrives and then discarded. It is never saved and never sent anywhere.
  • ADA has no internet access. The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of transmitting anything — not audio, not incidents, not analytics.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.
  • What ADA saves — a list of drone-like events it noticed — stays in the app's private storage on your device, and you can delete it.

What ADA accesses, and why

What Why What happens to it
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) To listen for drone-like sound while you have armed ADA Audio is held briefly in memory, analysed in short windows, and discarded. No recording is written to storage and nothing is transmitted.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) To show the required "armed" notification, and to alert you Stays on the device
Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE) So listening continues with the screen off, with a visible notification Not data
Vibration (VIBRATE) To alert you Not data

ADA requests no other permissions. It has no access to your contacts, location, photos, files, phone identity or network.

The microphone is used only while ADA is armed. You arm it deliberately; it never listens before that. While it is listening, Android shows a notification the whole time, and ADA does not restart itself after you disarm it or after another app takes the microphone.

What is stored on your device

When ADA notices a drone-like sound it saves a record of the event, never the sound:

  • when it started and ended, and how long it lasted
  • how confident the detector was, and the decision it reached (alerted you / stored quietly / ignored on purpose)
  • acoustic measurements — frequency, harmonics, loudness, signal-to-noise and similar numbers
  • which drone model the sound most resembled, if any, and how closely
  • the sensitivity settings in force at the time
  • whether the event came from the in-app Demo feature rather than the microphone

It also stores your settings (sensitivity, alert timing, appearance, and so on).

All of this lives in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. It is not uploaded, backed up to us, or shared with anyone, because there is nobody to share it with — the app cannot reach the network.

What ADA never does

  • It never saves your audio.
  • It never sends audio, incidents or usage data anywhere.
  • It never listens when it is not armed.
  • It has no advertising, no trackers, no third-party analytics SDKs.
  • It does not identify you, and does not build a profile.

Your control over your data

  • Uninstall: removing the app deletes everything it has stored on your device — incidents and settings alike. Because nothing is ever uploaded, that is genuinely all of it.

A control to clear the stored history from inside the app, without uninstalling, is planned; this page will be updated when it ships.

Drone model names

When ADA names a drone model it is reporting acoustic similarity to a known recording, not an identification of a specific aircraft or its operator. ADA cannot tell you who is flying a drone, where it is, or who owns it. It has no camera, no location access and no network.

Children

ADA is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Open data used by the detector

ADA's detector was trained on publicly available datasets, and the app bundles some recordings and photographs from them for its demo and reference features. These are credited in the app under Settings → Data sources & credits, as their licences (CC BY 4.0) require. No personal data is involved.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, this policy will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
