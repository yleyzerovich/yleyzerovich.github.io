Privacy policy Terms of use

Privacy

The apps are built to work on your device. In practice that means:

  • No accounts, unless a feature genuinely cannot work without one — none of the current apps require one.
  • No cloud storage of your video, photos, or location. What the app records stays on your phone.
  • Permissions are requested when a feature needs them, and the app explains why.
  • Each app has its own privacy policy, linked from its Google Play listing, covering exactly what that app accesses. Acoustic Drone Alert’s policy is published here.
